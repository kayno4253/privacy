SoundBridge Android service demonstration

Package: com.elne.soundbridge · version 0.5.0 (5). Actual Android emulator recordings. These clips show the foreground service interface and controls; they are not an end-to-end audio quality test.

Media playback / receiver

The user opens the receiver and starts receiving. The service remains visible through its Android notification.

Media projection / sender

Recording begins after Android screen/audio capture consent was granted by the user. The sender is waiting for a peer; the capture indicator and foreground notification are visible. The user stops the service from its notification and returns to the stopped sender screen. System audio is captured only after Android consent; protected content is not bypassed.
